# Pallavi Srinivas: Game Assemblies Lab — Spring 2026

## LLM-Driven NPCs — Game Assemblies Lab

An experimental NPC system exploring how large language models (LLMs) can enable characters to make context-aware decisions, adapt their behavior to changing environments, and express behaviors based on their identity and internal state.

**Project Overview:** This project investigates how LLMs can be integrated with traditional game AI systems to create NPCs that respond dynamically to their environment rather than following entirely predetermined behavior.

The NPC observes its environment through game-engine signals and uses this context to determine appropriate actions, dialogue, and behavior. The system combines LLM-based reasoning with structured game-AI approaches to support more adaptive and context-aware characters.

The final prototype places the NPC in a procedurally generated environment where its movement, actions, and dialogue adapt to environmental conditions and other external factors.

NPC identity can also influence behavior. For example, different identities such as a human, bird, or lion can produce different behavioral patterns, emotional responses, and actions within the same environment.

## Independent Study Final Deliverable

The project investigates how LLMs can be integrated with traditional game AI systems to create NPCs that respond dynamically to their environment rather than following entirely predetermined behavior.

The NPC observes its environment through game-engine signals and uses this context to determine appropriate actions, dialogue, and behavior. It leverages LLMs and can successfully navigate and meaningfully respond to a procedurally generated environment.

The NPC’s movement and dialogue dynamically adapt to environmental conditions and other external factors. Depending on the NPC’s identity (human, bird, lion, and so on), its behavioral patterns, emotional responses, and actions reflect that role across the changing environment.

The LLM drives the NPC to make logical and informed decisions, enhancing the user’s believability of the game and the world they are within.

## Research Questions

### Environment Perception & Context

- How does the NPC identify and contextualize the environment around them?
- Do different visuals (or visual abstractions) change how well an LLM-grounded NPC can reason about space and state?

### Game AI & LLM Integration

- How can LLM reasoning be combined with traditional game-AI architectures such as behavior trees and state machines?
- How can motivation, goals, and environmental context be represented in a way that supports reliable NPC decision-making?

### NPC Behavior & Identity

- How does an NPC's identity influence its interpretation of the environment and resulting behavior?
- How can LLM-driven decision-making contribute to more believable interactive characters?

### Technical Exploration

- Survey state-of-the-art technology that exists today for LLM-driven agents in games or interactive systems.
- Combine concepts pertaining to behavior trees, LLMs, and motivation.
- Primitives as interface between world and model: geometry, colliders, sound, navigation meshes, triggers, and other engine-level signals the NPC can “read.”

### Related work

The project was informed by existing approaches to interactive and AI-driven characters, including research and systems involving:
- Behavior trees and hierarchical AI
- LLM-based game agents
- Procedural environments
- Goal- and motivation-driven NPC behavior
- Context-aware interactive characters
